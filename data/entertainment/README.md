## What matters for an outing

### Cinema halls and screenings

MCine Trianon has third-party labels for an accessible entrance, adapted toilet and wheelchair parking. These are not a screen-specific wheelchair seating plan. For every cinema, ask for the exact screen, a space where you can remain in your wheelchair, adjacent companion seating, the full step-free route and usable toilet. Recliner/VIP seats do not establish transfer-free access.

MCine branch identity is recorded separately from old Star listings. [A March 2026 report](https://lexpress.mu/s/les-cinemas-star-ferment-definitivement-leurs-portes-555512) describes Star's permanent closure, while current cinema listings identify MCine branches, including La Croisette. Stale directory accessibility labels under Star branding are not transferred to MCine. Confirm the operator and screening rather than relying on old brand names.

The outdoor cinema operator lists three locations. Each is a separate record because surfaces, toilets and routes may differ. A listed venue does not guarantee a screening every day. Bring-your-own-chair, deck-chair and drive-in tickets do not prove wheelchair access; car size and sightlines may affect drive-in viewing. Confirm the exact site and date.

Caudan Arts Centre's operator reports an entrance ramp, lifts, theatre wheelchair lift and dedicated seating. It links to existing curated venue MU0002. Reserve an exact performance/screening position and confirm companion seating and lift operation; toilet and dedicated parking details remain unestablished by this source.

### Race venues and sports spectators

- **Champ de Mars:** the record identifies the Port Louis site. Its historical government source is not a current race schedule, operating-authority confirmation or spectator accessibility statement. Access claims about the Paris Champ de Mars Arena and a nearby Mauritian hotel were excluded.
- **Cascavelle Karting:** an activity-seller search listing was found, but product-page content could not be reliably retrieved. Current operation must be checked first. The older SpeedoMax name is not added as another venue without evidence of a separate site.
- **Côte d’Or:** the operator describes provision for physically challenged spectators in its stadium seating. That supports a general access-feature claim, not an allocated wheelchair bay or a verified route. A lift described for the VIP lounge is not assumed available to ordinary spectators.
- **Anjalay parking motorsport site:** the government archive search excerpt concerns a March 2026 refurbishment visit. It is a planning lead, not evidence that an operating circuit or accessible event has been completed.
- **Vallé mountain luge:** the seller reports no dedicated wheelchair facilities and says limited access may be possible at management discretion. This does not establish kart boarding, transfer method, controls, suitable equipment or an accessible spectator position. Confirm before booking.

For horse racing, motor racing and karting, spectator access and participation are separate. Being able to reach a reception area does not establish grandstand/paddock access or use of a vehicle. No adaptive kart, hoist, transfer assistance or track participation is confirmed in this batch.

### Bowling and other entertainment

Strike City Bagatelle has a generic third-party wheelchair-access label. Accessible lane approaches, ball ramps/adapted equipment, toilets and parking remain unconfirmed. General mall access is not automatically assigned to the entertainment activity.

Observation fields: observation_id, venue_id, feature, status, scope, source_id, checked_date and summary. Unknown rows record retrieval gaps, not sources stating access is absent. Operator general-seat claims are retained with their wording/scope rather than converted into a numbered wheelchair bay.

Source fields: source_id, title, url, evidence_type, retrieval_scope and checked_date. Cascavelle and Anjalay evidence is explicitly search-excerpt-only where full relevant text was unavailable. Source/check dates do not establish that facilities were working on a visit.
