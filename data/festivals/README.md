# Festivals in Mauritius — wheelchair access research

**Checked: 12 November 2026.** This first batch has **14 edition/component records across 12 festival series**, with **24 observations and 15 sources**. It includes past editions as research history and two advertised future events. It does not assert that all listed events took place or will recur.

## Read the levels

| accessibility_level | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| unknown | No adequate event-specific wheelchair access statement retrieved. Does not mean inaccessible. |
| venue_features_only | General venue facilities are documented, but event-day operation and allocated spaces are unconfirmed. |

These are evidence categories, not numerical ratings. **No event in this batch is certified fully accessible.** Event feature fields stay unknown even when venue-only features are reported in separate columns. Future evidence can support additional documented categories; do not assign high/medium/low scores by guess.

## Practical findings and limits

- **Japanese Film Festival — Kokuho:** the Embassy confirms the 1 November 2026 screening. Caudan Arts Centre separately lists an entrance ramp, lifts, wheelchair theatre lift, dedicated seating and trained assistance. Those venue statements do not confirm event allocation or an adapted toilet. Its curated venue link is MU0002.
- **Mystik, 28 November 2026:** the ticket page warns of limited parking and advertises garden/beach areas and seating. Reserved wheelchair bays, firm routes and adapted toilets are not established. Ticketbox and Sway show different hours; no time is encoded until clarified.
- **Grand Diwali Night, 14 November 2026:** advertised on a rooftop at Valentina Hall. Confirm a continuous step-free route and lift operation before booking; a rooftop is not automatically inaccessible.
- **Rang Barse 2026:** the listing restricts personal chairs. It does not explicitly state whether that covers or exempts mobility aids. No wheelchair ban or exemption is inferred.
- **Afric Vibes:** general venue parking is listed separately. Ordinary parking and movable seats do not establish wheelchair bays or wheelchair seating.
- **Festival Internasional Kreol:** three records represent different advertised 2025 locations/components of one festival edition. The programme names towns, not exact accessible entrances. These are not three extra festival brands or a 2026 programme.
- **Maha Shivaratree:** police traffic controls/general car parks do not prove a wheelchair route. Parking reserved for officials/service vehicles is not classified as wheelchair parking. Notice closure/transport periods are not treated as exact festival dates.
- **MAMA JAZ:** the official page confirms April 2026 and the 11th edition, but the linked programme PDF was unavailable. No 2024 venue is silently assigned to 2026.
- **Awake:** indexed ticket information gives Coteau Raffin/Le Morne; generic aggregator categorisation as Quatre Bornes was excluded. Its full-page retrieval was inconsistent, so this record is a date/location lead, not an access assessment.

“Accessible”, “welcoming”, free entry, general seating or the appearance of a wheelchair athlete at an event are not sufficient evidence of wheelchair access. No visitors or organisers have been contacted. No festival visitor accessibility reviews were verified in this batch.

The London Mauritius Open Air Festival and Seychelles' Festival Kreol were excluded from this Mauritius-location dataset. Historical Porlwi search snippets were not promoted into current access claims.

## Download and Power BI

| File | Grain | Key |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [events.csv](events.csv) | One edition/component at a venue, or an explicitly unresolved multi-venue edition | record_id |
| [accessibility_observations.csv](accessibility_observations.csv) | One finding/research gap with evidence scope | observation_id |
| [sources.csv](sources.csv) | One source URL | source_id |

Use UTF-8 and comma delimiter. Import events alone for a flat report. For evidence analysis, relate events[record_id] (1) to accessibility_observations[record_id] (*) and sources[source_id] (1) to accessibility_observations[source_id] (*), single-direction. Do not add an active event_source_id-to-sources relationship on top of this path; use a separate source query if identity lookup is needed.

Set edition_year to Whole number, start_date/end_date/last_web_check/checked_date to Date, and field_verified to True/False. Leave missing dates null. Everything else is Text. date_text preserves partial dates. start_date/end_date encode advertised calendar dates, not event times or verified opening/closing instants.

Count **DISTINCTCOUNT(events[festival_id])** for festival series and **DISTINCTCOUNT(events[record_id])** for edition/components. Filter edition_year and listing_status_as_of_check before planning. Status is frozen at the web-check date and does not update automatically.

These event records are separate from the 6,965 OSM place records and 35 curated place records. They are different grains; do not sum them as unique venues. related_curated_place_id, when populated, links to data/places.csv. That linkage is identity context and does not inherit event accessibility.

## Fields

| Field(s) | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| record_id | Stable edition/component ID, never reused or renumbered. |
| festival_id | Stable festival-series ID shared across editions/components. |
| festival_name, edition_year, component_name | Series, year and specific session/location component. |
| venue_name, region | Sourced venue/area or explicitly unspecified. Regions are practical outing areas. |
| start_date, end_date, date_precision, date_text | Supported advertised dates; precision day/day_range/month. Month-only dates are not converted to arbitrary first/last days. |
| listing_status_as_of_check | advertised_future, archived_edition or occurrence_reported. Archived means a past listing, not proof of attendance/occurrence. |
| accessibility_level | Evidence category defined above. |
| step_free_entrance, wheelchair_parking, accessible_drop_off, wheelchair_toilet, wheelchair_viewing_area, companion_ticket, assistance_booking | Event-specific statuses. All currently unknown; no venue claim is automatically inherited. |
| venue_ramp, venue_lift, venue_wheelchair_seating | General venue context only: unknown or operator_reported_yes. |
| related_curated_place_id | Optional key to the separate curated venue table. |
| event_source_id | Source establishing event identity/dates, not every access claim. |
| contact_or_booking_url | Public listing for follow-up; not a contacted/verified access service. |
| last_web_check, field_verified | Retrieval date and field-inspection flag. All field_verified values are false. |
| notes, questions_to_confirm | Evidence limits and useful questions before travelling. |

Observation fields: observation_id is unique; record_id and source_id are foreign keys; feature identifies the finding; status preserves unknown/operator_reported_yes/directory_reported_yes/clarification_needed/reported_limited/reported_yes/conflicting; scope distinguishes edition_component, venue_general and edition_traffic_plan; checked_date is retrieval date; summary paraphrases evidence. An unknown observation records a scoped retrieval gap, not an explicit source claim that access is absent.

Source fields: source_id, title, url, source_type, retrieval_scope and checked_date. Awake is explicitly search-excerpt-only for dates/location. General venue information is dated by retrieval, not an inspection or guarantee that facilities worked during the past event.

## Research updates

Preserve IDs and older editions. Add a new row for a new edition/venue component. Record event-specific details separately from permanent venue facilities and keep conflicting reports. Do not copy entire reviews, images or programmes into these files. Ask for entrance/drop-off location, firm route, adapted toilet, reserved viewing, companion policy and assistance arrangements for the exact edition.
